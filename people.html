Ahmet Enis Cetin

Professor · Electrical and Computer Engineering · IEEE Fellow

Ahmet Enis Cetin leads the group at the University of Illinois Chicago. His research spans inverse problems, signal and image processing, computer vision, and sensor systems. He received his PhD from the University of Pennsylvania in 1987.
