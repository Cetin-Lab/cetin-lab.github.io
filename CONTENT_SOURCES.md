# Content sources and editorial notes

Prepared November 16, 2026. The website is based on the public sources below and the user's additional roster information.

## Original source limitation

The requested Google Site, <https://sites.google.com/uic.edu/ahmet-enis-cetin/home>, redirected to Google sign-in. The user chose to proceed using public UIC and lab GitHub sources. The supplied HTML fragment was treated as content; stray tags and duplicate student lists were removed. No private Google Sites content was accessed.

- [UIC ECE faculty profile](https://ece.uic.edu/profiles/ahmet-enis-cetin-phd/): faculty role, research interests, selected grants, public email, office address, phone, portrait, and links to the CV.
- [Ahmet Enis Cetin's 2026 CV](https://ece.uic.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/351/2026/07/Ahmet_Enis_Cetin_CV2026.pdf): current PhD roster, graduation records, and 2025 NASA challenge recognition. The current-student list appears on printed page 28 (PDF page index 27); recent PhD graduates appear on printed page 27.

The home and research pages contain original short summaries rather than a verbatim reproduction of these pages. The Hadamard diagram is an original, exact 8×8 Sylvester sign-pattern illustration; it is not a reported experimental heatmap or prediction.
