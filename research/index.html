Research

Research Areas

The Cetin Lab investigates mathematical and computational approaches to signal and image processing, machine learning, and sensing, with particular emphasis on structured methods and computational efficiency.

01

Efficient Machine Learning

Hadamard transformsNeural networksEdge AI

Research in efficient machine learning examines structured transforms and nonlinear thresholding operations within neural network architectures. Areas of investigation include Hadamard representations, computationally efficient inference, and hybrid quantum–classical approaches to learning.

Selected ICML Publication
02

Wildfire Detection and Environmental Monitoring

Computer visionRemote sensingWildfire spread

Research in environmental monitoring addresses wildfire and smoke detection in camera imagery, satellite-based observations, and next-day wildfire spread prediction. Methods include transfer learning for visual detection and compact U-Net architectures integrating spectral and directional transform representations.

ShearFuse-UNet Preprint
03

Biomedical Signal and Image Processing

MRIInverse problemsNeural oscillations

Biomedical research includes magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) bias-field correction and instantaneous phase estimation in neural signals. Investigated methods include variational Hadamard-based networks for MRI correction and dual-branch architectures incorporating cosine-transform layers for neural phase estimation.

Neural Phase Estimation Publication
04

Sensing and Mechanical Condition Monitoring

Vibration analysisPhase estimationSensor systems

Research on mechanical sensing investigates transform-based signal analysis for condition monitoring. Work on gearbox vibration measurements employs dual-branch networks with Walsh–Hadamard layers to estimate instantaneous phase and examine changes in mechanical system behavior.

Gearbox Phase Estimation Repository
