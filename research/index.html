Our research

Structure meets discovery.

We study how mathematical models and efficient learning methods can turn complex measurements into useful information.

01

Efficient machine learning

Hadamard transformsNeural networksEdge AI

Transform-domain layers offer an alternative way to organize computation inside neural networks. Our work explores Hadamard representations, thresholding, and hybrid quantum–classical architectures, connecting signal-processing structure with practical learning systems.

Explore the ICML paper
02

Wildfire & environmental monitoring

Computer visionRemote sensingWildfire spread

We study visible-camera fire detection, satellite monitoring, and next-day spread prediction. Research ranges from comparing transfer-learning models for smoke detection to combining spectral and directional representations in compact U-Nets.

Explore ShearFuse-UNet
03

Biomedical signals & imaging

MRIInverse problemsNeural oscillations

Our biomedical work includes MRI bias-field correction and instantaneous phase estimation for neural signals. VHU-Net uses Hadamard representations for MRI correction; a separate dual-branch network uses cosine-transform layers to estimate neural phase.

Explore neural phase estimation
04

Sensing & machine condition monitoring

Vibration analysisPhase estimationSensor systems

Transform-based learning also supports mechanical signal analysis. Our gearbox research uses a two-branch network with Walsh–Hadamard layers to estimate instantaneous phase from vibration signals, helping characterize changes in machine behavior.

Explore the gearbox project
